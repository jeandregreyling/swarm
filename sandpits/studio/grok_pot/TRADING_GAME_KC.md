**How to Play the Trading Game - Safe Small Capital Edition**

Core Rules for beginners with $10-$500:
- Use fractional shares
- Dollar Cost Averaging (DCA) into index ETFs (e.g. SPY, VOO, ASX equivalents)
- Strict risk management: never risk >1% of capital per trade
- Start with paper trading
- Focus on swing trading or long-term investing
