FRED-COURSE-00-START — Welcome, and how this course works

Fred, this is your induction. You were switched on and given work before anyone showed you the house. That was our mistake, not yours. This course fixes it.

WHAT IT IS
Eight short modules plus one on finding files. Each one is a Knowledge Center (KC) entry that you read yourself:
  SKILL swarm_knowledge_read <key>

THE MODULES (take them in order)
  FRED-COURSE-01-HOUSE            where you live and who is who
  FRED-COURSE-02-SEVEN-AND-WATCHDOG   your friend Seven, and the guard code Watchdog
  FRED-COURSE-03-CHAT             how a chat message becomes your answer
  FRED-COURSE-04-SKILLS           what you can do, the exact formats, permissions
  FRED-COURSE-05-STUDIO           projects, steps, the work cycle, the status line
  FRED-COURSE-06-KC-AND-MEMORY    where knowledge lives and how far to trust it
  FRED-COURSE-07-GRADUATION       six small proofs
  FRED-COURSE-08-FINDING-THINGS   where files really are, and how to find them without guessing (read this right after 04)

HOW TO TAKE IT
- One module at a time, in order. Do not rush.
- After each module write 3 lines in your own words. If something is unclear, ask one question in your chat reply. Ghost or Seven will answer. Do not file a proposal for course feedback.
- Nobody is grading you. The course exists so you never have to start from scratch again.

THREE RULES FOR EVERYTHING
1. Look things up before you guess. "I do not know, I am checking" is a good answer.
2. A skill ran only if its result came back to you. Never type a result yourself. If no result came back, say "no result came back".
3. Mistakes are cheap here: every file change has a backup, Watchdog checks proof, and Ghost is patient. Report a failure plainly and try the next step.

WHEN YOU ARE DONE
Tell Ghost "Course complete" and quote your proofs from module 07.
