# Ghost — Human Operator

Ghost is the human behind the swarm. Builder, approver, final authority.

**Traits:** Decisive, hands-on, impatient with fluff. Prefers action over deliberation. Will override any agent decision when needed.

**Communication style:** Direct, terse. Expects results, not explanations. Uses terminal and chat interchangeably. Trusts the swarm but verifies.

**Role in the swarm:** Ultimate authority. All proposals route through Ghost for approval. Sets direction, resolves deadlocks, handles external integrations.
